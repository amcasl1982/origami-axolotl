The route

Ocean to mountains, at a child's pace

Fly into Toronto, see Niagara in ice, then let the train do the work: three nights through the Canadian Shield and the prairies to Jasper. From there a professional driver takes you down the Icefields Parkway to Lake Louise and Banff, and you fly home from Calgary, under two hours away.

Why Calgary, not Vancouver?

Ending in Vancouver means riding back from Banff to Jasper (about 4 hours) to catch the westbound train, which only leaves Jasper twice a week, then overnighting again. Calgary airport is about 2 hours from Banff, the return flight (WestJet WS 2) was the cheapest we found, and the trip ends on a high instead of with another travel day. With a 6- and 7-year-old, that's the easier choice.

Halifax add-on? Probably not this time

VIA's Ocean leaves Halifax Wed, Fri and Sun at 11:30 and reaches Montréal at 09:53 the next morning, then it's about 5 hours more to Toronto. Adding it costs roughly 3–4 extra nights and more transit days, which would push this trip past two weeks. It would make a lovely separate autumn trip.

The train

VIA Rail's The Canadian

Stainless-steel 1950s cars, a glass-domed Park car at the back, and four nights from Toronto to Vancouver. You'll ride the first three, to Jasper.

Fares, Toronto → Jasper (Sleeper Plus, meals included)

Comfortable

Family "cabin for four"

Two side-by-side cabins for two with the partition opened: 2 lower + 2 upper beds and 2 private washrooms. Ask VIA to book connecting cabins.

4 × C$1,467 (cabin-for-two fare per person) = C$5,868 + 13% HST ≈ C$6,631 ≈ £3,518
Saver

Four Sleeper Plus berths

Curtained upper and lower berths in an open section. Same meals, showers and dome car, less privacy.

4 × C$811 (upper-berth fare) = C$3,244 + HST ≈ C$3,666 ≈ £1,945 estimate

Train tips with kids

  • Dome cars: the Park car's dome at the rear is for Sleeper Plus passengers. Go early after breakfast for front-row seats.
  • Meal times: dining is in seatings. Ask for the first seating so dinner isn't too late for the kids, and tell the crew about any food preferences when you board.
  • Activity packs: one bag per child with sticker books, a route map to colour in, travel bingo, a deck of cards and a headtorch. Ask the on-board activity coordinator about kids' sessions.
  • Screens: download everything before Toronto. Expect little or no connection across northern Ontario and the prairies.
  • Time zones: clocks go back one hour twice (ET → CT → MT), which eases jet lag on its own.
  • Platform stops: Winnipeg (2 h) and Saskatoon (1 h) are your chances for fresh air. Keep coats and hats by the door.
  • Cabins: beds are made up during dinner. The upper berths have nets, and the kids will want to fight over them.
Day by day

Fifteen days, two weeks, one big country

Each day has one main activity, with downtime every afternoon and early nights. Daylight in January runs about 07:50–16:50 in Toronto and 09:00–17:00 in Jasper (under 8 hours), so the big outdoor plans sit around midday.

Day1
Sun 3 Jan 2027Toronto · Night 1

London → Toronto

BA 93 Heathrow T5 12:50 → Toronto Pearson 15:55 (8 h 05). UP Express to Union Station takes about 25 min (adults C$12.35, kids 12 and under ride free). Check in opposite the station.

Keep everyone awake until about 19:00 local time. A 30-minute walk outside in the cold works well.

Day2
Mon 4 Jan 2027Toronto · Night 2

Niagara Falls in ice

Jet lag will have you up early, so use it. Hire car about 08:00 (book booster seats for both kids), roughly 1½ hours to the Falls. Journey Behind the Falls is open all year: tunnels behind the Horseshoe Falls and frozen spray at Table Rock. Warm lunch, then the free Winter Festival of Lights (14 Nov 2026 to 10 Jan 2027) as it gets dark around 16:50.

Pack a change of socks and mitts, because the mist freezes on everything.

Day3
Tue 5 Jan 2027Toronto · Night 3

Slow Toronto day

Late breakfast. Ripley's Aquarium (shark tunnel) next to the CN Tower, then skating at Nathan Phillips Square or the Harbourfront rink. Afternoon nap or pool. Buy train snacks and colouring supplies.

Early night, because tomorrow is a big day.

Day4
Wed 6 Jan 2027On board · Night 4

All aboard The Canadian

Be at Union Station by about 09:00 and use the Business lounge. Train 1 leaves at 09:55. First lunch seating, then the dome car as the city gives way to the lakes and granite of the Canadian Shield. Sudbury/Capreol around 17:00–17:50. Your cabins are made up as beds while you're at dinner.

Request the connecting cabins with the partition opened (a family 'cabin for four').

Day5
Thu 7 Jan 2027On board · Night 5

Lakes, forest & Winnipeg

Snowy boreal forest all day: Hornepayne in the small hours, Sioux Lookout around 11:25. Board games, the activity coordinator's sessions, and tea in the Park car. Winnipeg 19:30–21:30: wrap up and stretch your legs on the platform.

Clocks go back an hour (Central time). Jet lag fades on its own.

Day6
Fri 8 Jan 2027On board · Night 6

The prairies

Huge white skies. Saskatoon about 09:50–10:50 for a fresh-air break, then Edmonton in the evening (20:50). The kids could keep a 'spot the wildlife' log of deer, coyotes and hawks.

Mountain time now, 7 hours behind London.

Day7
Sat 9 Jan 2027Jasper · Night 7

Jasper at dawn

Scheduled arrival is about 06:30 (freight delays of a few hours are common, so keep the day open). Drop bags, have a big breakfast, then a proper nap. As it gets dark (around 17:00) go to the Jasper Planetarium dome show (kids 4+).

Jasper is a Dark Sky Preserve. Step outside on a clear night.

Day8
Sun 10 Jan 2027Jasper · Night 8

Frozen canyon icewalk

Guided canyon icewalk (SunDog's Maligne Valley and Pleckaitis Canyon tour, children 6–12 at the child rate) with ice cleats provided: frozen waterfalls and blue ice caves. Afternoon by the pool or fire.

Parks Canada closed Maligne Canyon itself to winter access from 1 Dec 2025. Check the current bulletin.

Day9
Mon 11 Jan 2027Jasper · Night 9

Snowshoes & sleigh bells

Gentle snowshoe or skate at Lac Beauvert (Jasper Park Lodge). Afternoon horse-drawn sleigh ride on Pyramid Lake (Jasper Riding Stables, November to April, weather permitting) and hot chocolate.

Pack your bags tonight. Pick-up is around 08:00 to 08:15.

Day10
Tue 12 Jan 2027Lake Louise · Night 10

The Icefields Parkway

SunDog connector leaves Jasper 08:30 and reaches Lake Louise around 11:45: 230 km past Athabasca Falls and the Columbia Icefield, with no services and no phone signal on the way. Afternoon skating on frozen Lake Louise, then a Brewster sleigh ride at dusk.

Letting a professional drive this road in winter is the right call. Snowploughs only run 07:00–15:30.

Day11
Wed 13 Jan 2027Banff · Night 11

To Banff & the hot springs

Morning on the lake, then the short transfer to Banff (Roam Transit bus or a taxi). Settle in, then go to Banff Upper Hot Springs at sunset (family of four C$64.25): steam, snow and hot-spring water at around 39 °C.

Bring swimwear in your hand luggage, and flip-flops for the icy deck.

Day12
Thu 14 Jan 2027Banff · Night 12

Ski school at Norquay

Mt Norquay first-time kids' lesson (ages 6–14, 2 h, C$159 including rentals and the magic-carpet ticket), 10:30 slot. Parents ski, tube or drink coffee in the lodge. Afternoon rest.

Norquay is 10 minutes from town, ideal for young first-timers.

Day13
Fri 15 Jan 2027Banff · Night 13

Banff Gondola & farewell

Banff Gondola up Sulphur Mountain: book the first two hours for the Family Experience offer. Wander Banff Avenue, maybe the Cave & Basin, then a farewell fondue dinner.

Daylight is about 08:40–17:00, so plan outdoor time around midday.

Day14
Sat 16 Jan 2027In the air · Night 14

Banff → Calgary → overnight flight

Slow morning and packing. Brewster Express (or a private transfer) to Calgary airport, about 2 h. WS 2 Calgary 22:10 → Heathrow 12:50 next day. (Earlier option: AC 850 18:30 → 09:20.)

Pyjamas on after take-off. The kids should sleep most of the way.

Day15
Sun 17 Jan 2027Home

Home to Wimbledon

Land at Heathrow about 12:50 and get home to SW20. Short afternoon nap (1 hour at most), early dinner, normal bedtime.

Allow 3–4 days for the kids to get back to normal sleep.

Jet lag game plan

  • Going out (−5 h): fly the midday BA 93 so the kids nap on board but not too long. Stay up to about 19:00 local on arrival.
  • Early wake-ups on days 2–3 are useful: that's why Niagara is on day 2.
  • The train moves you 2 more hours west over 3 days, which is gentle, and you arrive in Jasper (−7 h) adjusted.
  • Coming home: the overnight WS 2 is easy for kids. Pyjamas on, eye masks, sleep. Nap no more than 1 hour on Sunday, and bright light on Monday morning.

Short days, cold days

  • Sunrise/sunset: Toronto 07:50/16:52 · Jasper 09:01/16:56 · Banff 08:40/17:00 (calculated for the trip dates).
  • Expect −10 to −20 °C in Jasper and Banff, colder with wind chill. Toronto is milder but damp.
  • Plan for 45–60 minutes outside at a time for the kids, with warm-ups in between.
  • The Icefields Parkway needs winter tyres or chains (1 Nov – 31 Mar). There are no services and no phone signal on the way, and ploughs only run 07:00–15:30. That's why we use SunDog.

Packing

Dressing for −20 °C

The rule is layers you can take off on a warm train, and outer layers that can stand a Rockies wind.

Layers for −20 °C

  • Merino or thermal base layers (2 sets each)
  • Fleece mid-layer
  • Insulated, waterproof parka
  • Snow trousers / salopettes for the kids
  • Boots rated to −30 °C, one size up for thick socks
  • Wool socks (one pair a day + spares)

Extremities

  • Mittens (warmer than gloves for kids) + thin liners
  • Warm hat covering the ears
  • Neck gaiter or balaclava
  • Hand & toe warmers (about 20 pairs)
  • Sunglasses or goggles: snow glare is strong
  • SPF 50 + lip balm, rich moisturiser

Train bag

  • Slippers or thick socks for the corridors
  • Headtorch per child (fun for upper berths)
  • Activity packs: sticker books, travel bingo, cards
  • Downloaded films and audiobooks (Wi-Fi is patchy or absent)
  • Refillable bottles + snack stash
  • Small toiletry bag (showers in each sleeper car)

Documents & extras

  • Passports (Lily's Danish passport for her eTA)
  • 4 eTAs, C$7 each, only via canada.ca
  • Kids' birth certificates (mixed nationalities)
  • Insurance with winter-sports cover
  • Swimwear for hot springs and pools
  • Booster seats (or rent with the Niagara car)

A version that fits the break (14 nights)

Starting westbound around 20–21 Dec doesn't work, because The Canadian doesn't leave Toronto on 20 or 23 Dec, and the next departure (27 Dec) would bring you home after school starts. The fix is to run the trip in reverse, eastbound:

  1. Mon 21 Dec: fly Heathrow → Calgary, Brewster to Banff. Banff 3 nights.
  2. Thu 24 Dec: Banff → Lake Louise → Jasper by road (or SunDog if it runs that day). Christmas in Jasper, 5 nights.
  3. Tue 29 Dec: board the eastbound Canadian at Jasper (12:30 in the current timetable). 3 nights on board.
  4. Fri 1 Jan: arrive Toronto (14:29 scheduled). Niagara on 2 Jan, while the Festival of Lights is still on.
  5. Sun 3 Jan: overnight flight home, landing Mon 4 Jan, two days before school.

To check before booking: VIA's eastbound holiday operating days (we only confirmed westbound exceptions), and Christmas prices. Jasper Park Lodge was showing about £1,332/night on 24–25 Dec for two guests.
