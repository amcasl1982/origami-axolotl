Day by day Drive times from OSRM without traffic · ★ = kids' favourite

Wed 30 DecFly overnight. British Airways nonstop from Heathrow at 22:20.
Overberg · Greyton
Thu 31 DecLand 12:10, collect the car. Supermarket shop, then about 1 h 50 over Sir Lowry's Pass to Greyton. A quiet New Year's Eve in the garden.
Fri 1 JanSlow day. Sleep off the flight, river paddling and walks under the oaks.
Sat 2 JanGreyton Saturday market. Then next door to Genadendal's mission village and museum.
3 – 4 JanVillage days. Cafés, the village pool, short trails and lazy garden afternoons.
Tue 5 JanVillage walks and the river. An easy stretch of the Boesmanskloof trail from the edge of Greyton, then a picnic and a paddle in the river.
6 – 7 JanSlow days in Greyton. Pool, garden, bikes and cafés in the village.
Fri 8 JanHome day. Garden, pool and a last braai in the village.
Sat 9 JanMarket, then to the sea. One more Saturday market, then about 2 h 20 to Kommetjie. Sunset on Long Beach.
Cape Peninsula · Kommetjie
Sun 10 JanKirstenbosch. The Boomslang treetop walkway (about 35 min); a Sunday summer sunset concert if tickets are out.
Mon 11 JanBoulders penguins★ Go at 08:00 (about 20 min), then Seaforth beach and fish and chips in Simon's Town.
Tue 12 JanCape Point★ Lighthouse, ostriches and baboons, then a picnic at Buffels Bay.
Wed 13 JanTable Mountain cableway★ The first car up if the wind is down, then a picnic and the evening concert at Kirstenbosch.
Karoo · Aquila Private Game Reserve
Thu 14 JanTo the safari (about 2 h 50). Camel rides at Imhoff Farm first, then via the Huguenot Tunnel. Check in, then the afternoon game drive.
15 – 16 JanBig Five days★ Morning and afternoon game drives, the Junior Ranger programme, the pool and marshmallows by the fire. All meals included.
Sun 17 JanFly home. Last morning drive and breakfast, then about 2 h 20 to the airport. Return the car; BA nonstop at 22:10.
Mon 18 JanHome. Land at Heathrow at 08:10.
