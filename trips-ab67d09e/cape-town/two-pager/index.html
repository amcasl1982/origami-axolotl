Day by day Drive times without traffic · ★ = kids' favourite

Mon 28 DecFly overnight. British Airways nonstop from Heathrow at 22:20.
Cape Peninsula · Kommetjie
Tue 29 DecLand 12:10 (BA nonstop), collect the car. Supermarket shop on the way, then about 55 min to Kommetjie. Settle in, sunset on Long Beach, early night.
Wed 30 DecBoulders penguins★ Go at 08:00 (about 20 min), then Seaforth beach and fish and chips in Simon's Town.
Thu 31 DecKirstenbosch + New Year's Eve. The Boomslang treetop walkway (about 35 min). The NYE concert there if tickets come up, otherwise a fire-pit evening at home.
Fri 1 JanSlow day. Camel and pony rides at Imhoff Farm (5 min), then the beach and rock pools.
Sat 2 JanCape Point★ Lighthouse, ostriches and baboons, then a picnic at Buffels Bay. Do Table Mountain instead if the wind has dropped.
Sun 3 JanTable Mountain cableway★ The first car up (the cabin spins), then on to Greyton via Franschhoek for lunch (about 2 h 30 of driving in total).
Overberg · Greyton
4 – 6 JanVillage days. Walks under the oaks, the river, cafés, and next door Genadendal's mission village. Lazy garden afternoons.
7 – 8 JanOptional: safari. Aquila (Big Five, ages 4+) is about 2 h 45 away. Do it as a day trip, or stay 1 night and come back.
Sat 9 JanGreyton Saturday market. A riverside picnic and the last log-fire evening.
Walker Bay · Hemel-en-Aarde
Sun 10 JanTo the valley (about 1 h 30). Grotto Beach in Hermanus in the afternoon: Blue Flag, lifeguards, gentle sand.
Mon 11 JanHermanus cliff path. Dassies, rock pools and ice cream. Big whales have mostly gone by January, so a whale boat isn't worth the money; dolphins are a bonus.
Tue 12 JanStanford + Klein River canoeing★ Paddle the calm river, then lunch in the village. Coot Club's lagoon is next door.
13 – 14 JanBeaches and farms. Voëlklip and Kammabaai beaches and lunch at a valley farm. Option: the Dyer Island boat from Gansbaai to see seals and penguins.
Fri 15 JanStony Point penguins. Drive Clarence Drive to Betty's Bay (about 50 min) for the quieter penguin colony.
Sat 16 JanFly home. Check out at 10:00, a long lunch in Hermanus, then about 1 h 30 to the airport for the BA evening nonstop.
Sun 17 JanHome.
